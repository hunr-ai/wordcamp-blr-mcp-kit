Step 2 · No coding agent?

Double-click instead

Same result, one launcher. You need Node.js 22 or newer; the launcher tells you how to get it if it's missing.

Windows

  1. Download the ZIP and open your Downloads folder.
  2. Right-click the ZIP, choose “Extract All…”, then open the new folder.
  3. Double-click Start-Windows.cmd. If Windows says it protected your PC, click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  4. Keep the window open. When it says your site is running, restart your AI app and try a prompt below.
Download ZIP

macOS

  1. Download the ZIP and double-click it in Downloads to unzip (Safari may already have done this).
  2. Open the wordcamp-blr-mcp-kit-main folder.
  3. Right-click Start-macOS.command and choose Open, then Open again. No Open button? Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click “Open Anyway”.
  4. Keep the Terminal window open. When it says your site is running, restart your AI app and try a prompt below.

What you're running

01

WordPress Playground

A real WordPress running on your laptop. No PHP, MySQL or Docker. It resets every time you stop it.

02

Official MCP Adapter

The WordPress.org plugin that turns your site's abilities into tools AI apps understand.

03

Your AI app

Claude, Cursor, VS Code and friends connect over MCP, the open standard for AI tools.

Safety

Built to be safe to play with

  • Everything stays on your laptop at 127.0.0.1. Nothing goes on the internet, and the site starts fresh every time.
  • The AI only sees the abilities your site chooses to share. There's a “delete all posts” ability in there, and the AI can't even see it.
  • Agents draft, humans publish. The AI signs in with an Application Password you can revoke in one click.
